Privacy Policy — DiceRoll: 3D Dice Roller

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Summary

DiceRoll does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, or contacts. Your dice rolls and settings stay on your device. The app does show ads, and the advertising provider collects some device information in order to serve them. Details are below.

Information we collect ourselves

None. DiceRoll has no servers of its own. Your selected dice type, skin, dice count, and haptics preference are stored only in your device's local storage and are never transmitted anywhere.

Information collected by advertising

DiceRoll shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Identifier for Advertisers)
  • Usage and advertising data (for example, which ads were shown or tapped)
  • Coarse location, derived from your IP address
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Google's handling of it is governed by their own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices about ads

  • App Tracking Transparency. On first launch, iOS asks whether DiceRoll may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. Every feature of the app works either way. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad personalization. Where required by law, DiceRoll shows a consent form on first launch. You can reopen it any time from Settings → Ad personalization inside the app.
  • Remove ads. Purchasing DiceRoll Pro removes all advertising, and with it the data collection described in this section.

Purchases

DiceRoll Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Whether you own the purchase is checked against Apple's servers using StoreKit.

Children

DiceRoll is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you, and to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal information ourselves, such requests are best directed to Google using the link above. You may also reset your advertising identifier at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
