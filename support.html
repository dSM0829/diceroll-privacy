Common questions

The dice don't vibrate

Check three things: the Haptics switch in the app's own Settings, then Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Vibration on your iPhone, and finally whether Low Power Mode is on — it disables all haptics.

How do I roll?

Swipe up anywhere on the table. Swipe harder to throw farther. You can roll up to three dice at once, and choose from D4, D6, D8, D10, D12 and D20.

I bought DiceRoll Pro but it's locked again

Open Settings → Restore purchases inside the app. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

Are the rolls fair?

Every die is a true polyhedron whose faces have identical area and sit the same distance from the center. The face-reading logic was verified across 240,000 randomized orientations.
